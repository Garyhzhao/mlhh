Program Design

A concise overview of goals, duration, structure, and evaluation.

Goals

  • Provide hands-on experience in real projects.
  • Deliver mentorship, workshops, and career guidance.
  • Assess and onboard top-performing interns to full-time roles.

Structure & Timeline

PhaseDurationActivities
Orientation1 weekOnboarding, intro workshops
Core Work10 weeksProject assignments, mentoring
Evaluation1 weekPresentations, feedback

Evaluation & Outcomes

  • Weekly mentor check-ins and mid-program review.
  • Final presentation and performance scoring.
  • Certificate of completion and referral letters for top performers.

Machine Learning in Human Health (MLHH)

Student Research Training and Internship Program at Fred Hutch Cancer Center, Seattle
Program Staff Apply Students Contact

Program Design

A platform for undergrads, graduate students, and recent graduates in Computer Science / Mathematics majors to receive research training of applied ML in health care.

Goals

  • Students receive research training by joining ML studies in real-world medical research.
  • Investigators foster students’ career development in ML and health care.
  • Everyone works towards pushing the boundary of biomedical science through training and innovation.

Training Deliverables

  • Project report and training record.
  • (potential) Conference abstracts and research articles.
  • (potential) Referrals to advanced research opportunities.

Application

Note: if you are a recent Bachelor's graduate, you are advised to jointly apply to the FHCC post-bacc program (https://www.fredhutch.org/en/education-training/fred-hutch-postbaccalaureate-scholar-program.html) to increase your chance of admission.

Note: recommendations from college representatives are also welcome.
